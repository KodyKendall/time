Characters (must stay identical in every plate; same fox cub, same kite):

- Milo: a small fox cub, storybook proportions (large head, about four heads tall). Rust-red base fur (#c9722f), darker saddle on back (#8a4a1e), cream chest, cream muzzle, cream tail tip. Tall alert ears, dark nose and eyes. His big tail is ~40% of his silhouette and shows mood: straight up when joyful, low and wet when sad. Anxious, mischievous, tender. Never anthropomorphised with clothes.
- The Kite: a simple red diamond kite, no face, wooden sticks faintly visible at edges, one long tail ribbon with small bows. The tail ribbon shows feeling: curling outward with want, whipping when angry, limp when hopeless, streaming straight and steady when at peace. A thin white string always leaves the kite toward its holder.

Characters (must stay identical in every plate; same girl, same kite):

- Wren: a ten-year-old girl. Dark brown hair in a ponytail. A mid-blue knee-length wool coat, dark brown tights, small dark brown boots. A long mustard-yellow knitted scarf whose ends stream in the wind (SHORTENED from the mending scene on: a square has been cut from it to patch the kite). Lanky ten-year-old proportions, longer face, no toddler chubbiness. Simple, appealing, storybook proportions. Small calm features. She never lets go first.
- The Kite: a simple red diamond kite, no face, wooden sticks faintly visible at edges, one long tail ribbon with small bows. The tail ribbon shows feeling: curling outward with want, whipping when angry, limp when hopeless, streaming straight and steady when at peace. A thin white string always leaves the kite toward its holder. From the mending scene on it carries a mustard-yellow scarf patch and a lumpy knot in its string.

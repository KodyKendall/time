# Milo — character brief

## Role
- Film(s): The Moon Thief (2-min short)
- Role in story: protagonist; a fox cub who steals the moonlight and learns to share it.
- Screen time: all 8 beats; close-up in beat 5.

## Personality
- **Anxious** — afraid of the dark; body language curls inward when scared.
- **Mischievous** — stealing montage is playful, quick, proud of himself.
- **Tender** — the turn in beat 5 is soft, not heroic; he learns, he doesn't conquer.

## Silhouette rules
- Must read at 120px (wide shots) and 600px (close-up).
- Key masses: tail = ~40% of silhouette, always visible; ears tall and alert.
- Must NOT look like: a dog, a cat, a wolf; no clip-art smoothness — watercolor fur.

## Palette
- base (rust): `#c9722f`
- dark (saddle / ears-back / socks): `#8a4a1e`
- cream (chest / muzzle / tail tip): `#f3e6c8`
- nose / eye: `#14100c`
- rim light (night): `#9fb6cc`

## Scale
- Body length ~300 design units nose-to-tail-base; tail ~180.
- Owl is ~0.8× Milo's seated height.
